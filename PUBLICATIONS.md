## OSF Open-Ended Registration

**Title:** *Türkiye Grid Foundation Model: Cross-Task Transfer and Calibrated Uncertainty for Multi-Horizon Forecasting in Türkiye's Electricity System*

- Status: Accepted and public
- Registration DOI: https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/FMCYQ
- Associated OSF project: https://osf.io/tz5pw

This OSF record documents the current exploratory evidence boundary, completed January 2024 EPİAŞ data audit, corrective generation-accounting audit, reproducibility principles, and the planned separation between exploratory development and a later frozen confirmatory preregistration. It is **not** the future confirmatory preregistration; the confirmatory holdout remains intentionally undefined and untouched.
