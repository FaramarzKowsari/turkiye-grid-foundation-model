# About Türkiye Grid Foundation Model

Türkiye Grid Foundation Model is a reproducible research-software project for studying cross-task temporal transfer in Türkiye's electricity system. The initial empirical design joins official EPİAŞ hourly consumption, source-level generation and day-ahead market-clearing price into one leakage-resistant forecasting problem.

The project is intentionally more than a model implementation. It includes a data contract, authenticated source client, chronological evaluation rules, probabilistic forecasting, conformal calibration, compute-budget safeguards, synthetic CI fixtures, a draft research protocol and a manuscript scaffold that refuses to invent results before the real-data study exists.

**Current status:** v0.1.0 research scaffold. No paper-level empirical claim is made yet.
