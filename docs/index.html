The research question

When does sharing help—and when does it hurt?

Electricity demand, renewable output and market price interact, but they do not obey identical dynamics. A shared representation can transfer useful structure; it can also create negative transfer during price shocks, renewable ramps or unusual demand periods.

That tension is the paper. The project does not assume a large neural model must win.

Research integrity first

Random time shuffling is excluded from the primary design. Holdout results cannot be used for retuning. Synthetic smoke tests are never reported as empirical evidence. A null result remains a result.
